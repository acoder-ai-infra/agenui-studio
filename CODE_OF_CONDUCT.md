# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community for everyone.
Contributors must communicate constructively, respect differing experience and
viewpoints, and focus criticism on ideas and code rather than people.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate
disruption, and publishing another person's private information. Maintainers may
remove content or participation that violates these expectations. Report conduct
concerns privately through the repository maintainers or GitHub's reporting
tools.
